What is peace. How do you define peace? If you’re talking about what you
can grow, what you can build, what you can share and breathe, then peace is
simply balance interpreted by the living world. This is the world that we
lost. The world as it was before the lights dimmed and the towers fell.

It exists now only in fragments of memory and soil we call home. You’ve
been living in a borrowed dream, citizen. This is the world as it exists
today... Welcome to the Ruins of Progress. We have only fragments of truth,
but what we know for certain is that at some point in the early twenty-first
century, all of mankind was united in consumption. We marveled at our own
efficiency as we gave birth to endless growth.

An insatiable hunger that devoured forests, oceans, and sky. We don’t know
who first stopped caring us or them  but we know it was us who burned
the Earth. At the time, we were dependent on power drawn from the sun,
and it was believed that light alone could save us. Throughout human
history, we’ve depended on the machine to provide. Fate, it seems, has
its own humor. The human spirit generates more renewal than any turbine,
and more warmth than any reactor. Combined with unity and soil, we found
all the strength we would ever need. There are gardens, endless gardens,
where cities once stood. For the longest time I wouldn’t believe it, and
then I saw the bloom with my own eyes. Watch the ruins split open as green
fingers reclaim the world. And standing there, facing the quiet beauty of
it all, I came to realize the truth. What is MAYHEM? Rebirth. MAYHEM is a
living revolution built to bring the world back to life to turn decay
into creation once more.
